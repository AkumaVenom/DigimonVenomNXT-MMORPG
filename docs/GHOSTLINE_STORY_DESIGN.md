# Super Xros: Ghostline — story design and authoring record

**Release:** Digimon Venom NXT v1.4.0  
**Campaign ID:** `xros_ghostline`  
**Spoilers:** This document reveals the culprit, the investigation sequence, and the ending. The player introduction and upgrade guide should remain spoiler-free.

## Scope and authorship

Ghostline is a third, independent private campaign using 31 distinct maps already shipped in the Super Xros world region: one peaceful service hub and 30 sequential field investigations. The map art is supplied Super Xros material. Campaign place names, people, dialogue, mysteries, and plot are original Venom NXT writing; they do not claim to reproduce another Digimon game's canon dialogue or story.

The atmosphere is cyber detective fiction: private casework, stolen identities, hostile remote commands, compromised account registries, preserved logs, independent witnesses, and a trusted information broker whose apparent pursuit of a hacker hides her own expanding control. The story consistently distinguishes a Digimon partner and tamer's personal agency from compromised terminal permissions. Victims retain relationships and choices while their tools issue commands they did not authorize.

The campaign brings the character's existing partners, current HP/SP, levels, stats, moves, bag, currencies, scan records, DigiLab, and DigiFarm. Authored rewards contain no replacement partners, training-level grants, or level-floor overrides. Level recommendations rise from a level 3 opening witness trial to one final battle against three level 99 partners.

## Central mystery and character arcs

Mara Vale is an established relief-network engineer and a credible local information broker. She genuinely helped tamers during the Long Blackout, when ordinary communications failed and people lost contact with their partners. She built Ghostline as a temporary emergency route. When survivors wanted to leave it, she decided that no one could be trusted with the risk of disconnection. She retained emergency authority, replaced consent with enforced shelter, and built the masked identity **Null Regent** to direct both the attacks and their investigation.

Mara guides the player into witnessed NPC tamer battles. A compromised postbattle recorder wraps each victory receipt in a supposedly protective recovery handshake. That request adds the witness's account to her authority network. A queued threat then makes the Regent seem to arrive just after the investigators, shifting suspicion toward an elusive external attacker. The effect happens after a first victory; repeated losses do not create additional hacked witnesses, and the story does not force victims through rematches for grinding.

The investigation gradually preserves contradictions instead of explaining them away: a second connection, a ticket renewed after revocation, a missing minute, an indefinite shelter policy, a shutdown order never acknowledged, a request rewritten from refusal to consent, and an attack message written before its supposed cause. The player eventually discovers that their own approved workflow is part of the intrusion. The campaign changes that workflow before asking more witnesses to trust it.

Mara is not revealed by a name coincidence or an old certificate. A clean recorder is demonstrated in field 24. A fresh, independently timed challenge is created in field 25. In field 26 — **campaign map 27** — Mara answers the live challenge using the same key that signs REGENT's current commands. Turning in that investigation earns the revelation. Earlier quest text raises questions without asserting the verdict.

Recurring supporting characters carry distinct responsibilities:

- **Iona** leads the case, preserves the uncomfortable evidence, retires the unsafe process, and keeps the final record accountable to its witnesses.
- **Jun** first administers the compromised workflow, then volunteers her own account to prove the clean recorder. Her correction changes a procedure, not only her wording.
- **Niko** begins as a courier with a small partner and grows into the witness network's captain. His return makes the enduring effect of the first intrusion personal. His final ledger records each witness's own recovery choice.
- **Bea and Sera** show why a refusal must be honored outside the compromised authority system. The first attempted control fails; they record that honestly, then validate independent revocation.
- **Oren** knew Mara during the relief effort. He preserves both her genuine earlier help and the evidence of her later abuse without letting either erase the other.
- **Rill** knowingly routed some threats before being compromised himself. He separates his own responsibility from commands later issued without him.
- **Cass** is a willing defender of Mara, not another hacked victim. After defeat he chooses to stand down, stays answerable to the witnesses, and refuses to treat old gratitude as permission for ongoing harm.

## Structure and chronology

Each ordinary field follows the same readable sequence: accept its case, defeat the marked quest tamer, return to the investigator, and confirm the report. The report awards a permanent numbered Access Proof and opens the forward portal. Previous fields remain available. The 30th field instead verifies the full investigation before the final boss, yielding exactly 30 authored battles in total: 29 quest-tamer trials and the final confrontation.

The hub offers a case guide, medic, normal shop, DigiLab contact, DigiFarm contact, and Mara as an intel contact. It has no wild or story battles. Mara appears in the hub and 16 of the 30 fields; she is deliberately absent from the others. Her initial field dialogue only knows what was already established, and a separate variant responds after that field's actual report is completed. Hub intel selects the newest completed evidence milestone, not a guessed state based on travel.

Every recurring person retains the same character identity and portrait in all roles. No map contains the same person twice. Mara's portrait is reserved exclusively for her, preventing another NPC from appearing to be a surviving Mara after the finale. Her intel role offers conversation only, with no healer or shop action.

## Hacking, revelation, and recovery phases

Fields 1–23 carry the first-victory hacking effect. Their result dialogue records an unexpected remote connection or changed permission. The saved hacked-witness markers describe historical incidents; completion does not erase that history. Their repeat conversation is the witness's current recovery testimony rather than a friendly invitation to reenact the intrusion.

Field 24 proves the replacement recorder emits no recovery handshake. Field 25 builds the live-key challenge. Field 26 reveals Mara on quest turn-in and retires her earlier friendly contact points, including the hub contact. Her optional field-26 confession is visible only after that earned revelation. A later optional field-28 appeal attempts to justify her control without reopening any friendly service. The final actor is her last appearance in field 30.

Field 27 proves independent revocation with Sera's own choice and isolates renewal requests while preserving normal traffic. Field 28 confronts Cass's chosen defense of the root. Field 29 audits every witness's independent exit route. Field 30 seals the full archive, verifies all 30 proofs, and permits the confrontation. Mara cannot use the witnesses' remaining access as a hostage condition because their routes are already independent.

The final team consists of normal Diaboromon, Chaosdramon, and Machinedramon at level 99. These are Mara's willing battle partners; the finale does not invent a free capture of their scan data. Losing preserves all earlier proofs and investigation work and permits another attempt against the same team.

## Ending and persistent reward

Winning the finale permanently closes Mara's root session and revokes her campaign access. **Every actor with character ID `mara_vale` is absent after completion**, including the hub, historical field contacts, optional late contacts, and final boss. The finale cannot be rematched. The automatic ending conversation is an authenticated transmission from surviving ally Iona, so no final-victory dialogue leaves a disappeared Mara visible or requires the player to approach a removed actor.

The four readable ending pages establish the revoked access, witnesses' own recovery choices, intact partner relationships, exact permanent reward, and return route. The final field's forward-position uplink opens only after completion and returns directly to the Backchannel. All earlier fields remain available for visits, with individual after-clear testimony and working services.

The reward is **Shiny Scan Mastery**, a permanent 20% multiplier to eligible Shiny wild-victory scan gain: the baseline 5% becomes **6%**. It is not an additional 20 percentage points, does not change the world's 1% Shiny encounter probability, does not award Shiny scans from authored NPC battles, and cannot be stacked through repeated completion claims. It follows the same relative-bonus convention as the existing Paradox mastery reward while targeting Shiny scan gain independently.

## Implementation contracts

- Content: `venom/common/xros_story_content.py`, a deterministic metadata builder that validates existing maps, existing opponents, portraits, levels, and prerequisite IDs without file IO or account changes.
- Layout: `venom/common/xros_story_layout.py`, 31 existing-map IDs and collision-validated placement points. Public world map geometry and encounter pools are not replaced by campaign metadata.
- Runtime: `venom/common/story.py`, per-character campaign state, quest acceptance and report capabilities, exact-prefix proof travel, private battles, reveal state, actor visibility, and secure ending transmission.
- Each field region is `ghost_01` through `ghost_30`; case contacts end in `_quest`, ordinary trials in `_trainer`. `ghost_26_quest` earns the revelation. `ghost_final_regent` requires all 30 case completions and proofs.
- Every forward route before the finale requires the current field's Access Proof. The final return uplink requires campaign completion. Retreat never consumes a proof.
- NPC hacking flags are first-victory historical effects on trials 1–23. Clean trials 24–29 and the final battle have no new hacking effect.
- All story-tamer trials are nonrepeatable after their first victory. Field training remains the repeatable practice route.
- `role_label` provides campaign-specific player labels such as CASE CONTACT, QUEST TAMER, INTEL CONTACT, and NULL REGENT without borrowing another campaign's terminology.
- Locked future journal chapters and final identity are redacted by the runtime until the corresponding investigation makes them available; content data does not rely on the client to enforce progression or privacy.

## Verification focus

The content tests validate the exact hub-plus-thirty scope, existing Xros-only maps, unchanged public catalog, ascending levels, ordered case prerequisites, permanent proofs, all forward/backward portals, completion-only return uplink, exactly 30 authored battles, real-party reward policy, normal opponent IDs, stable recurring portraits, Mara's reserved identity and removal flags, earned map-27 revelation, clean-recorder flag transition, evidence-dependent intel, readable dialogue page lengths, unique authored chapter text, surviving narrator epilogue, and explicit failure for damaged catalog references.

Runtime, acceptance, scan, layout, audio, and UI tests are maintained separately so structural authoring tests are supplemented by real quest actions, battles, travel, saves, multiplayer isolation, visible ending behavior, and native client rendering.
